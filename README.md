About the Project

A historical 2D action game featuring a brutal and dynamic combat system inspired by Guns, Gore & Cannoli 2. The game tells the tragic story of a family torn apart against the backdrop of a collapsing empire. At the center of the plot are twin brothers from the impoverished noble Vorontsov family, who find themselves on opposite sides of the barricades.

Story & Lore

The events span from the fronts of World War I (1916) to the height of the Russian Civil War (1919). By the finale of the story, both brothers are 22 years old.

The Vorontsov family scraped together their last funds to provide an officer's education for only one of their sons. This single decision forever divided their fates and worldviews:

Pavel Vorontsov (White Army)

Having received his education at the family's expense, he became a lieutenant (poruchik) in the Imperial, and later the White Army. He embodies discipline, cold calculation, and loyalty to his oath.

Combat Style: Elegant, tactical, and precise.

Ranged Weapon: Revolver.

Melee Combat: Classical fencing and deadly duels with a shashka (cavalry saber), implemented through a special fighting-game style camera.

Pyotr Vorontsov (Red Army)

Deprived of privileges, he was sent to the front lines as a common infantryman. Ashamed of his noble origins among peasant sons, he passed himself off as an innkeeper's boy. Influenced by his comrades in the trenches, he embraced Marxism, which bred a deep class hatred directed at his own noble heritage. He eventually became a Red Army soldier.

Combat Style: Aggressive, with a heavy focus on high mobility.

Ranged Weapons: Sawed-off shotgun and grenades.

Melee Combat: Brutal and visceral bayonet executions driven by accumulated rage.

The Climax: Brother Against Brother

The final scene of the game is a blind clash between the brothers on the battlefield. Amidst the chaos of war, they do not recognize each other.

The Blind Fight: An intense skirmish where neither knows who they are fighting.

The Cutscene: A powerful knockback strike strips away their combat disguise/gear. A brief, heavy dialogue takes place as they catch their breath.

The Conscious Fight: The duel resumes, but now each brother knows exactly who he must kill.

Two Alternate Endings (Player's Choice):

Pavel Wins: He kills Pyotr, spits on his traitorous brother's body in disgust, and walks away in silence.

Pyotr Wins: He kills Pavel, shakes his head with heavy regret, takes his brother's weapon (as a trophy and a symbol of defeating the past), and leaves.
